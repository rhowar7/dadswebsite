Siding Installation & Replacement

Serving Jarrettsville, Forest Hill, Bel Air, Fallston, Aberdeen, Havre de Grace, Abingdon and Edgewood — free on-site estimates.

Siding by a Contractor Who's Been Doing It for 46 Years

New siding is the fastest way to change how a house looks from the street — and the surest way to stop water getting where it shouldn't. Walter handles full replacements and repairs, and dries in new additions so they're ready for the weather.

Walter's Home Improvement is licensed and insured (MHIC #16421), based in Jarrettsville, and has served Harford County homeowners for over 46 years. Every job starts with a free on-site estimate — Walter looks at the actual space, talks through what you want, and gives you a price. No pressure, no obligation.

What's included

  • Vinyl siding installation and replacement
  • Fiber cement siding
  • Stucco and wood siding
  • House wrap, flashing and trim
  • Soffit, fascia and gutters
  • Siding for additions and dormers

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Walter gets back to you within one business day.
  2. Free on-site estimate. He comes out, looks at the job, and gives you a price.
  3. Schedule the work. You get a start date and a realistic finish.
  4. The work gets done. You deal with Walter throughout — the same person who gave you the price.
