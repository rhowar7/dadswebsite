Bathroom Remodeling by a Contractor Who's Been Doing It for 46 Years

A bathroom is the smallest room in the house and one of the hardest to get right. Walter has been remodeling them across Harford County for over four decades — from pulling out a tired tub-and-tile surround to rebuilding the room from the studs.

Walter's Home Improvement is licensed and insured (MHIC #16421), based in Jarrettsville, and has served Harford County homeowners for over 46 years. Every job starts with a free on-site estimate — Walter looks at the actual space, talks through what you want, and gives you a price. No pressure, no obligation.

What's included

  • Shower and tub demolition and replacement
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile — floors, walls, shower surrounds
  • Walk-in showers
  • Vanities, countertops and fixtures
  • Plumbing and lighting updates
  • Exhaust fans, drywall and paint to finish

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Walter gets back to you within one business day.
  2. Free on-site estimate. He comes out, looks at the job, and gives you a price.
  3. Schedule the work. You get a start date and a realistic finish.
  4. The work gets done. You deal with Walter throughout — the same person who gave you the price.
