Questions? We Have Answers

Real questions from Maryland homeowners — answered honestly.

➕ How long does a typical bathroom or kitchen remodel take?

Bathroom remodels typically take 4–6 weeks depending on scope. Kitchen remodels run 6–10 weeks. We'll give you a specific timeline after the estimate walk-through. Most projects stay on schedule — that 98% on-time completion rate matters here.

➕ What if something goes wrong during the project?

We handle it immediately — at no cost to you. That's why we offer a 5-year guarantee on all work. If you're unhappy with anything, we fix it. We're a local, family-owned business that's been here 46 years and plans to be here 46 more — we can't afford to cut corners.

➕ How much will my project cost? Do you have financing?

Costs vary by scope — we'll give you a detailed estimate with no surprises. We accept all forms of payment: cash, check, credit card, and debit card. If you need payment flexibility, we can discuss options during your estimate.

➕ Will you work around my schedule? What about nights/weekends?

Yes. We can work early mornings, evenings, or weekends if that works better for you. Just let us know during the estimate. We're flexible and want to minimize disruption to your life.

➕ Do you pull permits? Who handles that?

We handle all permits and inspections as part of the project. You don't have to worry about it. Permits protect you, and we know the process inside and out.

➕ How do I get a free estimate?

Call (443) 829-5946, text us, or fill out the form on our estimate page. We'll respond within one business day and set up a time that works for you. The estimate is free, detailed, and no-pressure. Walter personally walks through your project so you know exactly what to expect.
