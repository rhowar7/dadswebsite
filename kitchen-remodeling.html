Kitchen Remodeling by a Contractor Who's Been Doing It for 46 Years

A kitchen remodel touches every trade at once — cabinets, counters, floors, plumbing, electrical. Having one licensed contractor carry the whole job means one estimate, one schedule and one person answering the phone.

Walter's Home Improvement is licensed and insured (MHIC #16421), based in Jarrettsville, and has served Harford County homeowners for over 46 years. Every job starts with a free on-site estimate — Walter looks at the actual space, talks through what you want, and gives you a price. No pressure, no obligation.

What's included

  • Cabinet installation and replacement
  • Granite, quartz and laminate countertops
  • Tile and plank flooring
  • Backsplashes and mosaic tile
  • Sink, faucet and appliance hookups
  • Lighting, drywall and paint

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Walter gets back to you within one business day.
